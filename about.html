ABOUT ISIBINDI GBV

Compassion, dignity
and the courage to act.

Creating awareness and helping people find information and support around gender-based violence.

OUR PURPOSE

A community where
everyone feels safe.

Gender-based violence affects individuals, families and communities. Access to clear information and supportive, non-judgemental guidance can help people understand their options.

Isibindi GBV is presented here as a non-profit organisation concept for the WEDE5020W website project. This website prototype is designed to communicate respectfully, make support information easier to find and encourage safer help-seeking.

01

Dignity

Every person deserves respect and to be heard without blame.

02

Safety

Information should support informed choices and safer next steps.

03

Empathy

Respond with care, patience and without judgement.

OUR COMMITMENT

Information with
care and purpose.

The site uses plain language, clear navigation and private, optional enquiry fields. Before launch, the organisation should review all content and confirm its real services, referral partners and contact details.
